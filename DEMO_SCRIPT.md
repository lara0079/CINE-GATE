# Three-minute demo structure

## 0:00-0:18 - Problem

Film teams manage likeness, voice, music, footage, and artwork rights across fragmented records. A release can proceed while a permission is missing, expired, channel-limited, or silent about synthetic use.

## 0:18-0:38 - Product

Introduce CINE-GATE as an agentic rights-discovery and human-review workflow. AI identifies possible assets and explains findings, but never invents consent or changes the deterministic outcome.

## 0:38-1:08 - Cleared scenario

Load the cleared trailer. Show two declared assets, explicit synthetic-use permission, channel scope, evidence references, `CLEARED`, the rights matrix, and readiness metrics.

## 1:08-1:38 - Blocked scenario

Load the expired-likeness scenario. Show the blocking finding, the corrective guidance, and the disabled approval button.

## 1:38-2:12 - Corrected revision

Choose **Create corrected revision**, update the validity date or permission record, and run the review again. Show revision 2 in the same case and the preserved revision history.

## 2:12-2:38 - Human accountability

Record a named human decision on the corrected record. Show the workflow timeline and checksum verification.

## 2:38-2:55 - Release package

Download the release-package ZIP and show the evidence JSON, rights-matrix CSV, printable report, and manifest with file checksums.

## 2:55-3:00 - Closing

CINE-GATE helps production teams discover, check, correct, explain, and document rights conditions before release while preserving accountable human review.
