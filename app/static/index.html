CONTROL BOUNDARY

No autonomous clearance

Agents cannot invent permission, authenticate documents, override a blocked result, or replace a qualified rights reviewer.

01

Discover

An advisory agent identifies rights-bearing material implied by the action description.

02

Check

A deterministic policy checks status, use, channel, territory, dates, commercial, modification, synthetic-media, minor-subject, attribution, and evidence metadata.

03

Explain

Gemini can explain recorded findings but cannot change the deterministic outcome or invent permission.

04

Resolve

A named human records a decision, or creates a corrected revision when the supplied record must change.

PRODUCT BOUNDARY

A focused rights workflow, not a legal oracle

CINE-GATE evaluates supplied metadata and produces a reviewable rights matrix, evidence packet, release package, checksum, and printable report. It does not verify a contract's authenticity, decide ownership, or expose any unrelated proprietary architecture.
